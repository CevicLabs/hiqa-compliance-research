# HIQA compliance research

How do care providers in Ireland keep track of their HIQA documents?

CevicLabs is part of Enterprise Ireland's New Frontiers programme. We're researching how care providers in Ireland manage their compliance paperwork, and where it gets hard.

## What we're looking at

Every designated centre is registered and inspected on its own. Each one needs current policies, training records, fire safety records and staff vetting, and a registration renewal pack sent to HIQA six months before its registration runs out.

We want to understand, from the people who do this work:

- how you know what's current, what's expiring and what's missing
- how long it takes to get ready for an inspection or a renewal
- what you use today, and what it doesn't do.

## What we're not doing

We're not selling anything. We want to understand the problem properly before building anything. We're not connected to HIQA or the HSE.
